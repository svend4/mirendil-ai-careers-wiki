# Product adoption review

## Cadence

Раз в 1-2 недели для active internal beta

## Purpose

Проверить, ускоряет ли продуктовый workflow реальные research decisions и не скрывает risk states.

## Required inputs

- Usage and adoption metrics
- Time-to-decision metrics
- Trace viewer feedback
- Approval friction notes
- Open UX/product incidents

## Decisions this review should make

- Promote workflow to more users.
- Fix usability/reliability blocker.
- Change product metric or instrumentation.

## Outputs

- Product decision memo
- Updated launch readiness status
- Backlog items for product/platform/design

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- Review без decision или owner.
- Status update вместо evidence-based discussion.
- Missing evidence не превращается в action item.
- Повторяющиеся incidents не попадают в [anti-patterns/remediations](../300-anti-patterns-remediations/README.md).
