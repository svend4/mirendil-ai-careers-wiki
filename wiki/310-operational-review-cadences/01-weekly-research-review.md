# Weekly research review

## Cadence

Еженедельно, 45-60 минут

## Purpose

Синхронизировать active experiments, negative results, blockers и decisions по research roadmap.

## Required inputs

- Open experiment briefs
- Eval reports from last week
- Failed or inconclusive runs
- Compute usage notes
- New research questions

## Decisions this review should make

- Continue, stop, narrow or expand experiment.
- Promote result to capstone or release review.
- Open backlog item for platform/eval/data gap.

## Outputs

- Decision records for material changes
- Updated research agenda items
- Follow-up owners and dates

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- Review без decision или owner.
- Status update вместо evidence-based discussion.
- Missing evidence не превращается в action item.
- Повторяющиеся incidents не попадают в [anti-patterns/remediations](../300-anti-patterns-remediations/README.md).
