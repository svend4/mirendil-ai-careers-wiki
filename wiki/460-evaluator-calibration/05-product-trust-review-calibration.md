# Product trust review calibration

## Audience

Product Development, Design Engineer, Platform reviewers

## Goal

Align judgments on trace comprehension, approval friction and blocked state visibility.

## Calibration exercises

- Observe same user/reviewer task.
- Label confusion points.
- Classify approval card completeness.
- Compare severity for feedback cases.

## Metrics

- Severity agreement
- Evidence lookup success
- Time-to-decision interpretation
- Blocked-state recognition

## Output

Product trust rubric, example cases and UX follow-up taxonomy.

## Cadence

Run before creating release-grade datasets, after major rubric changes, and after repeated disagreement appears in [operating reports](../380-operating-report-templates/README.md).

## Quality bar

- Reviewers label independently before discussion.
- Disagreement examples are kept as training material.
- Adjudication rationale is written, not only discussed.
- Updated rubric links back to [labeling guidelines](../450-labeling-guidelines/README.md).
