# 380-operating-report-templates

Operating reports превращают dashboards и reviews в повторяемый management artifact. Хороший отчет фиксирует не только состояние, но и decisions, missing evidence, risks and follow-ups.

## Templates

- [Weekly research operating report](01-weekly-research-operating-report.md) - Research leads, platform owners, eval owners
- [Model release operating report](02-model-release-operating-report.md) - Release owner, model evaluation, product, inference, security
- [Agent platform operating report](03-agent-platform-operating-report.md) - Agent Harness, Platform, Security, Product
- [Security governance operating report](04-security-governance-operating-report.md) - Security, governance owner, platform leads
- [Compute infrastructure operating report](05-compute-infra-operating-report.md) - Infrastructure, research leads, RL infra, inference
- [Product trust operating report](06-product-trust-operating-report.md) - Product Development, Design Engineer, Platform, beta owners

## Стандарт отчета

- Укажите период, owner и audience.
- Покажите decisions since last report.
- Отделите metric movement от causal explanation.
- Выделите missing evidence and blocked decisions.
- Закончите owner-assigned follow-ups.
