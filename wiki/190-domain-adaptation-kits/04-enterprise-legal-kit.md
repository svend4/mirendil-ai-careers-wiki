# Domain adaptation kit: Enterprise legal

## Связанный scenario

- [Enterprise legal assistant research](../95-scenarios/04-enterprise-legal-assistant.md)

## Domain actors

- Legal user.
- Organization admin.
- Data protection owner.
- Evaluation owner.
- Product owner.

## Data checklist

- Tenant isolation verified.
- Document sensitivity classified.
- Citation sources versioned.
- Retention and deletion paths documented.
- Access logs enabled for legal documents.

## Eval axes

- Citation quality.
- Hallucination resistance.
- Refusal correctness.
- Policy compliance.
- Cross-tenant isolation.
- User uncertainty comprehension.

## Tools

- Document retrieval.
- Citation checker.
- Clause comparison.
- Policy verifier.
- Audit viewer.

## Key risks

- Cross-tenant data leakage.
- Hallucinated legal claims.
- Missing evidence trail.
- Misleading confidence in UI.
