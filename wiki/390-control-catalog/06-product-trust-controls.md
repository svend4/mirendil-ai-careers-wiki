# Product trust controls

## Risk covered

Product UI hides risk states, approvals lack context or metrics reward unsafe automation.

## Preventive controls

- Blocked/risky states appear in primary workflow.
- Approval card includes impact, risk, rollback and evidence.
- Product metrics include trust and decision quality.

## Detective controls

- Product trust dashboard.
- High-severity feedback aging.
- Approval request completeness checks.

## Corrective controls

- Hold beta expansion.
- Improve trace summary or blocked-state UX.
- Change metric or launch scope.

## Evidence

- Launch readiness memo
- Trace viewer feedback
- Approval request sample
- Product trust report

## Audit questions

- Как reviewer увидит, что control реально включен?
- Какой dashboard, trace, event или artifact подтверждает работу control?
- Кто владеет remediation, если control сработал?
- Какой [decision tree](../330-decision-trees/README.md) помогает принять следующий шаг?
