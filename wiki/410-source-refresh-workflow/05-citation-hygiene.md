# Citation hygiene

## Purpose

Сохранить аккуратную работу с источниками и не копировать длинные внешние тексты.

## Steps

- Use source links instead of long quoted blocks.
- Prefer paraphrase with section context.
- Keep exact dates for dated material.
- Separate external claim from local analysis.

## Evidence

- Sources page update
- Short citation note
- No long copied passages
- Snapshot date

## Gate

Citation hygiene passes when reader can trace source without seeing copied source text.

## Review checklist

- Source is primary where possible.
- Dates are explicit for time-sensitive claims.
- Affected wiki pages are listed.
- Changes are reproducible through generator and checks.
- If uncertainty remains, claim is marked as assumption or inference.
