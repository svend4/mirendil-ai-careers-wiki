# Source claim review

## Purpose

Проверить, какие утверждения можно оставить, изменить или перенести в assumptions.

## Steps

- Разделить claims на factual, interpretive and inferred.
- Проверить dates, names, role titles and links.
- Пометить inferred conclusions explicitly.
- Не усиливать claim beyond source wording.

## Evidence

- Claim table
- Source link
- Confidence level
- Affected wiki sentence

## Gate

High-impact factual claim requires source and date.

## Review checklist

- Source is primary where possible.
- Dates are explicit for time-sensitive claims.
- Affected wiki pages are listed.
- Changes are reproducible through generator and checks.
- If uncertainty remains, claim is marked as assumption or inference.
