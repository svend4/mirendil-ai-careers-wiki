# Job role diff workflow

## Purpose

Обновить роли и позиции, если careers/job board изменился.

## Steps

- Сравнить список roles, titles, locations and publication dates.
- Отметить added, removed and renamed roles.
- Не удалять старую роль молча: пометить snapshot status.
- Обновить role pages, assignments and onboarding links.

## Evidence

- Role diff table
- Affected role folders
- Updated position index
- Decision record for structural change

## Gate

Role diff accepted only if navigation and links remain valid.

## Review checklist

- Source is primary where possible.
- Dates are explicit for time-sensitive claims.
- Affected wiki pages are listed.
- Changes are reproducible through generator and checks.
- If uncertainty remains, claim is marked as assumption or inference.
