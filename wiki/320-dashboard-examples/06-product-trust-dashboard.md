# Product trust dashboard

## Audience

Product Development, Design Engineer, Platform

## Operational question

Помогает ли продукт принимать research decisions быстрее и безопаснее?

## Core widgets

- Time-to-understand trace
- Time-to-approval decision
- Users hitting blocked states
- Workflow completion rate
- Feedback themes by severity

## Expected actions

- Fix UX blocker
- Improve trace summary
- Change launch scope or beta cohort

## Data quality checks

- Widget has owner and refresh cadence.
- Missing metadata is shown explicitly.
- Dashboard links to evidence packet, trace, eval report, incident or backlog item.
- Thresholds are documented and reviewed after incidents or releases.
